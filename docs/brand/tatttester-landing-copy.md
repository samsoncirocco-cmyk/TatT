## How it works

Section heading: **Sure, then ink. In that order.**

**1. Design it.**
Describe your idea and our design engine, Image2Ink, drafts it in the style
you want. Refine until it looks like the thing in your head.

**2. See it on your body.**
The AR preview places the design on your own arm, shoulder, wherever — at
real size, in real light. Move. Check the mirror. This is placement without
commitment.

**3. Wear it as a temp tattoo.**
Export your design as a temporary tattoo and live with it. See it at work,
at the gym, on day four when the novelty wears off. That's when you really
know.

**4. Book the artist.**
Still love it? We match you with artists whose portfolio fits your design.
Your artist gets a clean stencil, refines it with you, and books you in.

## FAQ

**What if I get it and hate it?**
That's exactly what TattTester exists to prevent. You'll have seen the
design on your body in AR, worn it as a temporary tattoo for days, and
approved your artist's refined version — three chances to change your mind
before anything is permanent. Most people adjust something along the way.
That's the system working.

**How real does the AR preview look?**
It places your design on your actual body at true size, following the
curve of the placement, in your lighting. It's a serious preview, not a
sticker filter — and the temporary tattoo step is there for the final,
in-the-mirror-for-a-week check.

**How long can I wear the temporary tattoo?**
Days — long enough for the honeymoon to end. Day one is exciting. Day four
is honest. If you still like it on day four, that means something.

**Do I have to use the AI design, or can I bring my own idea?**
Start either way. Type a rough idea and refine it, or bring something
specific you've been sitting on. Either path ends the same: a design tested
on your body and refined by a real artist.

**Will a real artist actually work from this?**
Yes — that's the point. Artists receive your design as a clean stencil and
refine it for skin. We match you with artists whose portfolio genuinely
fits your design's style, and the final line work is always theirs.

**What does it cost?**
You pay for design generation and a booking fee when you book your artist.
The tattoo itself is priced by your artist, as always. Join the waitlist
and you'll see exact pricing before you pay anything.

**I'm not ready to commit to anything. Can I still use it?**
Yes. Testing is the product. Design, preview, wear a temp, sit with it —
booking only happens if and when you're sure. No pressure is a feature.
