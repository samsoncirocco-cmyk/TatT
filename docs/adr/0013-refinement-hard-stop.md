# One refinement round, hard stop

After the reveal: pick → "most not you" tap → one refinement question → single regeneration → handoff. The bot never offers further iteration — unbounded refinement repositions the product from confidence layer to AI toy people play with and never book from. The hard stop is a product decision disguised as a technical one: it tells the user the artist is where refinement happens.

## Considered Options

- **Bot-judged convergence with a cap** — rejected: the bot never feels confident enough to force the handoff.
- **User-controlled free refinement** — rejected: worst outcome for conversion and for supply-side trust (artists don't want designs iterated to death).

## Consequences

The canvas editor is the sanctioned next stop for last-mile visual tweaks after the hard stop; the brief then goes to the artist. Bot handles the ambiguous/emotional phase, canvas handles the specific/visual phase — two user modes, two tools.
