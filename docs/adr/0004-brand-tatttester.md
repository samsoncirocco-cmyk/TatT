# Brand: TattTester is the primary mark; Image2Ink is a feature, not the brand

"TatT" was always a working name (per the original pitch deck). We chose
**TattTester** as the primary consumer-facing mark because the business sells
trust to people scared of getting a bad tattoo, so the name must promise the
test ("try before you ink"), not describe the mechanism. The brand is
consumer-first (nervous first-timers at the front door) with a separate
"for Artists" pitch on the pro side.

## Considered Options

- **Image2Ink** as the brand — rejected: describes the pipeline, not the
  promise; sounds like a file converter. Retained instead as the feature name
  of the design generator and as a second marketing door (image2ink.com) for
  the "I want a tattoo but don't know what" customer state, funneling into
  TattTester.
- **Tatt-T** as the primary mark — rejected: spoken aloud it collides with
  "tatty" (UK/AU slang for shabby), the worst possible read for a trust brand.
  Kept as DBA/nickname only.
- **TatT / TatT Tester** stylized capitalization — rejected: fails the radio
  test (invisible when spoken, awkward to type, splits search).

## Consequences

One brand, two messages: the trust angle ("test it before it's forever") and
the Image2Ink discovery angle (which showcases artist matchmaking) are ad/page
level variants, NOT separate brands — an early-stage two-brand split would
divide traffic, reviews, and word-of-mouth. Domains tatttester.com, tatt-t.com,
and image2ink.com were registered at Cloudflare on 2026-07-20. Trademark
screening is still open.
