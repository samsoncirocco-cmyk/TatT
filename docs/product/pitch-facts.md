# Pitch facts

This file is the factual gate for decks, pitch scripts, investor pages, and
fundraising copy. If a claim is not here or linked to evidence, verify it
before publishing it.

## Safe product claims

- TatT is pre-launch.
- The repository contains a working conversational intake and design-session
  flow.
- The system can generate multiple tattoo directions through provider-routing
  code.
- TatT can perform static photo compositing and a manually positioned
  live-camera overlay.
- TatT can match designs and preferences against artist data.
- The repository contains artist browsing, booking, reservation, deposit,
  Stripe Connect, availability, and calendar-integration paths.
- The platform holds a large scraped artist and portfolio dataset.

## Claims requiring careful wording

| Topic | Accurate wording | Do not claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Placement | User-positioned photo or camera preview | Anatomical tracking, depth mapping, ±2 cm accuracy |
| Generated work | Direction or brief for an artist to refine | Finished or production-ready tattoo design |
| Artists | Scraped profiles available for discovery | Onboarded or participating artists |
| Payments | Payment flows are implemented for launch readiness | Real transaction volume or revenue |
| Product availability | Pre-launch public product surfaces | Established commercial launch |
| Matching | Hybrid matching machinery and real artist data | Proven conversion lift without measured results |
